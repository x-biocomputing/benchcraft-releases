# BenchCraft

A laboratory programming workspace by CogWorks, with AI assistance and PyLabRobot. This repository distributes preview installers.

[Download BenchCraft for macOS (Apple silicon)](https://github.com/x-biocomputing/benchcraft-releases/releases/download/v0.0.1/benchcraft-desktop-mac-arm64.app.tar.gz)

1. Extract the archive and move **BenchCraft Beta** to Applications.
2. Open BenchCraft and sign in with Google or an email code.
3. Your verified account receives **$50 in one-time testing credits** automatically. No invitation or payment details are required to start.

Subscriptions, one-time top-ups and account access are available at [benchcraft.cogworksai.com](https://benchcraft.cogworksai.com). You can also use your own supported model provider.

The preview is unsigned; macOS may ask you to allow it in Privacy & Security. Instrument compatibility depends on the PyLabRobot driver and your lab setup.

Windows and Linux downloads are in progress. Checksums and build details accompany each release.
